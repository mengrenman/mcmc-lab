An interactive companion to MCMC from Scratch

Learn Markov chain Monte Carlo by watching it work

MCMC turns a hard integral into an average over random samples. Each module here puts one idea under your control: step the sampler, change a knob, and see what happens to the chain and its error bars. Every algorithm is written from scratch, with no libraries.

How to read the labs

One color convention everywhere

  •   Gray line: the exact answer, when one is known.
  •   Blue, then orange and aqua: what your simulation produced.
  • ✓ Accepted and ✗ Rejected mark individual MCMC decisions.

Hover over any chart for exact values.

Suggested path

  1. Monte Carlo integration: why random sampling works and where it breaks down.
  2. Metropolis: the core algorithm, plus the diagnostics you will use everywhere.
  3. HMC and Gibbs: two ways past the random walk.
  4. Bayesian inference: where most people meet MCMC in practice.
  5. Ising model: a real physics problem where the choice of algorithm decides everything.
  6. Annealing and replica exchange: MCMC as an optimizer, and how to escape local minima.
  7. Lattice gauge theory: the algorithms behind lattice QCD, on a toy you can run.

Coverage

Every chapter from 2 to 6. Book §6.4 runs on a two-dimensional U(1) toy model rather than full four-dimensional QCD, with the same algorithms.
